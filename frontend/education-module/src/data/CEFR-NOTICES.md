cefr.json converts these CSV records to JSON; headwords, parts of speech and
levels are retained. This adapted data remains available under the source terms;
the C1-C2 portion is CC BY-SA 4.0. Levels describe headword/part-of-speech records,
not every possible sense of a word. Source inaccuracies may remain.

Local OPTIMA translations are supplemental and are not provided by CEFR-J or
Octanove. Missing translations are explicitly marked in the interface.

## Definitions and Relations

Free Dictionary API https://dictionaryapi.dev/
Selected entries are fetched on demand. Each response's sourceUrls and license
are displayed alongside its content. Synonyms and antonyms remain grouped by
part of speech, and absent data is not inferred.
